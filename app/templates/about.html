Our story

Why we built EventLink Zambia

Finding out what is happening in your city often means scrolling through social media posts, chasing organisers for ticket details, and paying in ways that leave no record. Booking a planner for a wedding or graduation usually depends on word of mouth.

EventLink Zambia puts all of that in one place. Clients discover public events and buy tickets securely, or request planning support for weddings, graduations, corporate functions, and private celebrations. Planners get a professional profile and a simple way to manage bookings and sell tickets.

Our mission

To make every Zambian event easy to discover, safe to pay for, and simple to plan.

  • One place for concerts, festivals, and celebrations
  • Verified tickets with one-time QR codes
  • Trusted, administrator-approved planners
