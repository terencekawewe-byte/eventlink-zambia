Terms and conditions

EventLink Zambia provides a platform for discovering approved public events, buying tickets, and requesting event planners. Creating an account means you agree to give accurate information and to keep your password private.

Planners may not offer services publicly until an administrator approves the profile. Event submissions stay unpublished until they are approved.

Ticket prices are set by the organiser and confirmed on the server. A ticket is valid only after the payment status is verified. Clicking a payment button does not by itself mean money has moved.

Clients may cancel a pending planner request at any time. An accepted request can be cancelled up to 48 hours before the event start. Completed bookings cannot be cancelled in the app.

If a published event is cancelled, ticket and payment records are kept. A refund is recorded only after someone confirms it was completed outside EventLink.
