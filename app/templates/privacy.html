Privacy policy

EventLink Zambia stores the account details you submit, including your name, email address, phone number, and a password hash. Passwords are never stored in readable form and are never shown to administrators.

Booking requests, orders, tickets, and notifications are visible only to the people involved and to administrators who need them to operate the platform.

The application does not ask for mobile-money PINs, card PINs, or card security codes. Do not send those details through the contact form.

Uploaded posters and portfolio images are stored as image files with generated names. Contact messages are kept so the team can reply.

For privacy questions, use the contact page.
