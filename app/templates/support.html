Tickets are created only after the payment is verified. On the checkout page, choose MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, Zamtel Money or Card and confirm the payment before the reservation time runs out. A failed or pending payment does not produce a valid ticket.

New planner accounts stay pending until an administrator approves them. You can log in to see the status, but planner tools stay locked.

Open Payment history and submit a refund request. The request is tracked in the system. It is not marked complete until an administrator records an external confirmation.
